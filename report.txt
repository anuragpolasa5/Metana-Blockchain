3. SUMMARY
================================================================================

Total Gas Savings:
  - Loops sumArray:     1,497 gas saved   (~4.6% reduction)
  - Loops findMax:      4,147 gas saved   (~12.0% reduction)
  - AlienFactory create: 49,122 gas saved (~14.2% reduction)
  - Factory deployment: 132,717 gas saved (~19.3% reduction)

Key Insight:
  Storage operations (SLOAD/SSTORE) dominate gas costs. The biggest wins come
  from reducing the NUMBER of storage writes (AF-04: removing a redundant array
  push saved ~20,000 gas) and PACKING variables into fewer slots (AF-01: 4 slots
  → 1 slot saved ~60,000 gas on construction).

  For loops, the savings are more modest on small arrays because the overhead
  per-iteration (unchecked, ++i) saves only 5-200 gas each. The memory cache
  (GL-01) provides the largest single improvement by converting N cold SLOADs
  (2100 gas each) into N cheap MLOADs (3 gas each).
